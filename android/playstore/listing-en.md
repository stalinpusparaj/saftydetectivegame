## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Safety Detective Town is a bilingual (English + Tamil) 3D game that helps children aged 4-7 practise safety THINKING, not just rules to memorize.

Playing as their own detective -- Anil the squirrel, Minnu the kitten, or Kanmani the calf -- your child solves six short cases (2-4 minutes each) with Inspector Gaja, a wise elephant mentor, and Constable Vaalu, a clumsy monkey sidekick. Watch for Nari the Trickster, a sneaky jackal whose tail always gives him away.

For your child, say: "Let's go solve a safety case together!"

THE SIX CASES
1. The Kitchen Clues (Home) -- find 5 hidden hazards hiding among safe look-alikes, then fix them.
2. Park Patrol (Park) -- predict what happens next, then pick the safe choice instead.
3. Knock Knock, Who's There? (Home, door) -- look for clues through a peephole, then call a grown-up.
4. The Video Call Twin (Home, tablet) -- spot a trickster on a video call asking for secrets.
5. Rewind at the Pool (Pool) -- rewind a story and change one moment to keep everyone safe.
6. Call 112! (Street) -- dial 112, answer the operator, and look around for landmarks.

Cases unlock in order, and a short "Daily Patrol" reviews earlier lessons before each new one -- so the thinking sticks.

WHY PARENTS LIKE IT
Every case trains a real skill: spotting danger among safe-looking things, predicting consequences, asking a trusted grown-up when unsure, rewinding a choice to make it safer, and practising India's emergency number, 112. Your child earns stickers and costume pieces for their Detective Notebook, plus a star rating for each case solved.

PARENT CORNER
Hold one button for 3 seconds and solve a simple sum to open the Parent Corner. There you can change language, voice, music, sound and motion settings, set how many cases play per session (1-5), see what your child is learning with tips to talk about at home, and reset progress any time.

PLAYS OFFLINE
After the first visit, the whole game works fully offline -- no internet needed for story time, a car ride, or anywhere else. You can also try it first with no install at stalinpusparaj.github.io/saftydetectivegame/.

YOUR CHILD'S PRIVACY
We built this with privacy first:
- No personal information is collected about children or adults.
- No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking.
- No special device permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.
- The Android app asks only for the standard notification permission, used solely for one optional, local, once-a-day reminder to come back and play if the game has sat unopened a while. This happens fully on the device -- no account, nothing sent anywhere -- and you can turn it off any time in Android Settings > Apps > Notifications, just like for any app. It is the only runtime permission this app requests.
- Progress is saved only on your child's own device, and you can delete it any time.
Read our full privacy policy (English and Tamil) at stalinpusparaj.github.io/saftydetectivegame/privacy.html.
