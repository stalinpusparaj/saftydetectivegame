## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Safety Detective Town is a bilingual (English + Tamil) 3D browser game built for children aged 4 to 7. Instead of asking kids to memorise rules, it trains safety THINKING: spotting real dangers among safe look-alikes, predicting what happens next, learning to ask a trusted grown-up when unsure, rewinding a story to change one risky moment, and practising calling India's emergency number 112.

SIX CASES, UNLOCKED IN ORDER
1. The Kitchen Clues (Home) - find 5 hidden hazards among safe look-alikes, then fix them.
2. Park Patrol (Park) - predict what happens next, then pick the safe choice instead.
3. Knock Knock, Who's There? (Home, door) - look for clues through a peephole, then call a grown-up.
4. The Video Call Twin (Home, tablet) - spot a trickster on a video call asking for secrets.
5. Rewind at the Pool (Pool) - rewind a story and change one moment to keep everyone safe.
6. Call 112! (Street) - dial 112, answer the operator, and look around for landmarks.

Before each new case, a short "Daily Patrol" reviews earlier lessons so nothing is forgotten.

MEET THE CAST
Play as your own detective - Anil the squirrel, Minnu the kitten, or Kanmani the calf. Learn from Inspector Gaja, a wise elephant mentor, and Constable Vaalu, a clumsy monkey sidekick. Watch out for Nari the Trickster, a sneaky jackal in disguise whose tail always gives him away.

PARENT CORNER
Behind a parent gate (hold a button for 3 seconds, then solve a simple sum), a grown-up can change language, voice, music, sound, and motion settings, choose how many cases play per session (1 to 5), see what the child is learning with tips to talk about at home, and reset progress.

REWARDS
Children earn stickers and costume pieces for their Detective Notebook, plus a star rating for every case.

WORKS OFFLINE
After the first visit, the game is installable and works fully offline - no internet needed to keep playing. You can also try it with no install at all: https://stalinpusparaj.github.io/saftydetectivegame/

YOUR CHILD'S PRIVACY, DONE RIGHT
- Collects NO personal information about children or adults.
- No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking.
- No special device permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.
- The Android app asks only for the standard notification permission, used solely to show one optional, once-a-day, fully on-device reminder to come back and play. Nothing is sent anywhere, and a parent can turn it off anytime in Android Settings > Apps > Notifications.
- Progress is saved only on your child's own device, and a parent can delete it at any time.
