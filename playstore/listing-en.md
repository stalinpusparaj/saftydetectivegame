## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Safety Detective Town is a free kids safety game and bilingual (English + Tamil) learning game for children aged 4 to 7. This preschool safety game and child safety app trains real safety thinking skills instead of asking kids to memorise rules: spotting hazards among safe look-alikes, predicting what happens next, learning to ask a trusted grown-up when unsure, rewinding a story to change one risky moment, and practising calling India's emergency number 112.

SIX DETECTIVE CASES, UNLOCKED IN ORDER
1. The Kitchen Clues (Home) - a hazard-spotting game: find 5 hidden hazards among safe look-alikes, then fix them.
2. Park Patrol (Park) - predict what happens next, then pick the safe choice instead.
3. Knock Knock, Who's There? (Home, door) - a gentle stranger-danger game: look for clues through a peephole, then call a grown-up.
4. The Video Call Twin (Home, tablet) - spot a trickster on a video call asking for secrets.
5. Rewind at the Pool (Pool) - rewind a story and change one moment to keep everyone safe.
6. Call 112! (Street) - an emergency number game: dial 112, answer the operator, and look around for landmarks.

Before each new case, a short "Daily Patrol" reviews earlier lessons so nothing is forgotten.

MEET THE CAST
Play as your own detective - Anil the squirrel, Minnu the kitten, or Kanmani the calf. Learn from Inspector Gaja, a wise elephant mentor, and Constable Vaalu, a clumsy monkey sidekick. Watch out for Nari the Trickster, a sneaky jackal in disguise whose tail always gives him away.

BUILT FOR INDIA, BILINGUAL BY DESIGN
A Tamil kids game and English kids game in one: switch between English and Tamil anytime, for children and parents across Tamil Nadu and all of India. This bilingual English-Tamil kids app practises India's own emergency number, 112, not a generic hotline.

PARENT CORNER
Behind a parent gate (hold a button for 3 seconds, then solve a simple sum), a grown-up can change language, voice, music, sound, and motion settings, choose how many cases play per session (1 to 5), see what the child is learning with tips to talk about at home, and reset progress.

REWARDS
Children earn stickers and costume pieces for their Detective Notebook, a star rating for every case, a Detective Rank that only ever goes up, and badges for daily play streaks.

A FREE EDUCATIONAL GAME THAT WORKS OFFLINE
No ads, no in-app purchases - completely free. After the first visit, this offline kids game is installable and works fully offline, no internet needed to keep playing. Try it with no install at all: https://stalinpusparaj.github.io/saftydetectivegame/

YOUR CHILD'S PRIVACY, DONE RIGHT
