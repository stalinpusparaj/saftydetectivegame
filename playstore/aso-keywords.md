# ASO keyword research — Safety Detective Town

Keyword research done 2026-10-31 to optimize the Play Console store listing
(short/full description) and store tags. Not official Google ranking data —
Google/Apple don't publish ranking-weight formulas; this follows ASO vendor
best practice (AppRadar, AppTweak, Moburst), checked against this app's real
features so nothing here overstates what the game does.

## Fact check: does Google Play have a free-text "tags"/keyword field?

No. There is no hidden keyword box. Play Console's only tag mechanism is
**Store settings → App category → Manage tags**: up to 5 tags picked from a
curated list of ~150 genre-level tags (e.g. "Puzzle", "Casual" — not
free-text). Google's crawler otherwise derives relevance purely from the
**title**, **short description**, and **full description** text. That's why
the keyword work below is entirely about wording those three fields well,
plus picking the 5 best-fit tags.

## Tags selected (Store settings → Manage tags)

Casual, Hidden object, Interactive story, Logic puzzle, Puzzle-adventure —
chosen because they describe real mechanics in the game (Case 1 is a
hidden-object hazard hunt, every case is a short interactive story with
choices, Case 2/5 are predict-the-outcome logic puzzles), not just popular
tags with no relevance.

## Core keywords (used in title / short description)

kids safety game, safety game for kids, child safety app, safety games for
children, learning game for kids 4-7, preschool safety game.

## Secondary keywords (woven into full description)

stranger danger game, hazard spotting game, emergency number game, bilingual
kids game, offline kids game, no ads kids game, free educational game,
toddler learning game, detective game for kids, safety thinking skills.

## India / Tamil-specific keywords

Tamil kids game, bilingual English Tamil game, India emergency number 112,
Tamil learning app, kids app India, Tamil Nadu kids education.

## Competitor note

"Sheriff Labrador Safety Games" (BabyBus-style, ages 3-6) is the closest
competitor already on Play and iOS. No dedicated India-market bilingual
Tamil kids-safety app was found — a genuine positioning gap this app's
English+Tamil toggle and India's 112 emergency number fill.
